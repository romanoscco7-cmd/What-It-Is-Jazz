About the Band

What It Is Quintet brings together five young jazz musicians, blending classic and contemporary sounds for an unforgettable experience. From intimate cafés to grand stages, our passion for jazz shines through every performance.

We specialize in creating memorable moments through our unique interpretation of jazz standards and original compositions, bringing fresh energy to every venue we grace.

Warm tone, modern lines, and deep bossa nova sensibility.

Roman has been playing music his whole life and puts hard work and practice into what he does. He's a jokester at times and is always making his friends giggle.

Alessandro Lasaine

Nathan Schneider

Alto Saxophone
